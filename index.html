About

My work sits at the meeting point of architecture, urban planning and water. I study how cities can rejuvenate and manage their surface water resources, from lakes, ponds and drains to historic stepwells, through better institutions, policies, community participation and technology.

My doctoral research developed a planning framework for the rejuvenation of urban water bodies in NCT Delhi, combining Integrated Lake Basin Management (ILBM) with collaborative planning. I have worked on internationally funded projects with Newcastle University and Cardiff University, and I teach research methods and urban design studios at the School of Planning and Architecture, New Delhi.

Education

  1. 2019 - 2025

    Doctor of Philosophy, Department of Physical Planning

    School of Planning and Architecture, New Delhi

    Thesis: Planning Approach for Rejuvenation of Urban Water Bodies: Case of NCT Delhi. Supervised by Prof. Rabidyuti Biswas.

    Assessed the institutions, policies, participation, information, technology and finances of ILBM and collaborative planning through stakeholder and community engagement and focus group discussions, using descriptive and inferential statistics (normality tests, correlation and regression). The study found institutions, participation and policies to be the weakest parameters and proposed a planning framework with strategies and recommendations.

  2. 2016 - 2018

    Master of Architecture in Urban Regeneration

    Faculty of Architecture and Ekistics, Jamia Millia Islamia, New Delhi

    Thesis: Rejuvenation of Water Areas (Ponds and Drains): Case of Aligarh City. Supervised by Ar. and Planner Sandeep Kumar Tyagi. First division with distinction (83%).

    Examined the Aligarh smart city proposal through an urban resilience lens and proposed design and policy solutions for degraded and encroached water bodies, developed with local stakeholders.

  3. 2011 - 2016

    Bachelor of Architecture

    Thesis: Mathura, A City of Heritage: Centre for Culture before Vishram Ghat. A design thesis studying the Yamuna River along the Mathura stretch. First division with distinction (76.5%).
